# Test fixtures

These files are instrumentation inputs, not production media assets.

* `hdr-colors.mp4`, `hdr-colors.yuv`, `hdr-colors.json`: synthetic BT.2020 HLG patches generated
  by `scripts/hdr-fixture.py`, with known nonlinear RGB values for colour checks.
* `fixture.mp4`: generated color bars and audio for decoding, editing and export.
* `person.png`, `person.mp4`: a crop of supplied horizontal reference frame 0752
  and its short encoded derivative, used to check segmentation and range timing.
* `stabilizer.png`, `shaky.mp4`: a supplied-reference crop and a controlled-motion
  derivative for geometric tracking.
* `speech.wav`: `test_speech.wav` from the pinned
  [Qwen ASR samples](https://github.com/antirez/qwen-asr/tree/924694251d9e0f18e5d86bbd06aa3ab5f870002d/samples).

Supplied reference material retains its original provenance. The synthetic
screenshots in the repository show test media rather than personal recordings.

* `time-vfr.mp4`: eight synthetic solid-color frames at known nonuniform timestamps,
  with B-frame reordering, generated by `scripts/time-fixture.py`.

* `hdr-ramp.mp4`: lossless Main10 HLG gray ramp, generated by `scripts/hdr-ramp.py`,
  for full export precision and highlight-headroom validation.

* `halation-reference.json`: 18 fixtures and 3,687 sampled pixels evaluated by
  `scripts/halation-reference.py` from YouRec `faf1815`'s highlight extraction,
  Gaussian blur and composite equations. Includes integral/odd reductions,
  small-highlight phases, blur downscaling, tiny images, flash colors, intensity
  and linear half-float headroom. These are CPU equation fixtures, not captures
  of the Apple GPU output.

* `noir-reference.json`: 24 independent fixtures from `scripts/noir-reference.py`
  for the pinned Noir shader: contrast, black crush, blue shadows, intensity,
  extended linear values and frame-seeded half-float grain in both orientations.
  CPU equations do not replace actual Apple GPU comparison.

* `sixteen-mm-reference.json`: 24 independent fixtures generated by
  `scripts/sixteen-mm-reference.py` from the pinned 16mm shader. Includes paired
  Gaussian half-float sampling, correlated color/monochrome grain, weave,
  rounded borders, one-pixel axes and extended-color frames.

* `stabilization-shake.mp4`: two seconds of deterministic random block texture,
  translated at 4.1/3.3 Hz and encoded at 60 fps with a 440 Hz audio track.
  Reproduce with `scripts/stabilization-fixture.py` (Python standard library and
  FFmpeg). No personal or third-party footage is used. Stabilization tests also
  use `time-vfr.mp4` and a rotation-tagged copy of the generated shake fixture.
